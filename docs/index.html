ReBLACK-XBOX

Public source and evidence archive for the BLACK original Xbox reconstruction.

1. Xbox reconstruction

The repository contains 13,675 function candidates and one XBE entry-point source, with one evidence note per source. The C and x86 assembly are reverse-engineered reconstructions, not recovered original source.

The recorded candidate ranges matched byte-for-byte after relocation replay. Archived reports note two boundaries without independent confirmation and 16 ObjDiff cases in the review band. These static results do not prove Xbox linking, runtime, or gameplay.

Reconstruction and documentation were prepared with AI assistance under human project direction and review.
